# Privacy Trajectory

When `/privacy` is invoked, preserve all trailing text as task input; otherwise
request the feature and sensitive-data scope.

1. Read `../skills/privacy-review/SKILL.md`.
2. Inspect fields, purposes, transformations, stores, logs, recipients, access,
   backups, retention, exports, deletion, and user control.
3. Rank minimization and lifecycle gaps from evidence.
4. Keep review read-only unless controls are requested; do not expose personal
   data or claim legal compliance.
5. Return the data-flow inventory, risks, controls, and policy questions
   concisely without inventing behavior.
